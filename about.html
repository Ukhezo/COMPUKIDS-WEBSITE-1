About us

We are an App Development Company based in Durban South Africa

We take pride in creating amazing apps!

Our mission

To help South African schools, small businesses and start-ups go digital with apps that are simple, affordable and built to work on any phone, even with slow data.

What we do

  • Websites and online shops
  • Android and iPhone apps
  • Data reports and automation
  • Learning games for schools

Why choose us

  • Local team that understands local customers
  • We use 10 programming languages and pick the best one for the job
  • Every project is tested before it goes live

How we work

  1. Listen: we learn what your business needs.
  2. Design: we sketch the screens and agree on them with you.
  3. Build: we code the app in the right language for the job.
  4. Test: automated checks catch problems before your customers do.
  5. Launch & support: we publish your app and keep it running.
